Couple Task Manager

Last updated: January 23, 2026

Couple Task Manager ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by Couple Task Manager.

By accessing or using our Service, you signify that you have read, understood, and agree to our collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with the functionalities of Couple Task Manager, we collect specific information when you register and use the application.

A. Personal Information

  • Name: To display your profile to your partner.
  • Email Address: Used for authentication, account recovery, and household invitations.
  • Date of Birth: Used to display birthday reminders or specific household features.

B. User Content

We collect the content you create and share within your "Household" to facilitate synchronization between partners:

  • Tasks and Lists: Titles, descriptions, due dates, and status of tasks.
  • Meal Plans and Recipes: Information about meals, ingredients, and cooking instructions.
  • Household Data: Information linking you to your partner.

C. Device Permissions and Media

The app requests specific permissions to function correctly. You may grant or deny these permissions in your device settings.

  • Camera and Photos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): We request access to your photo gallery or camera to allow you to upload profile pictures or images associated with recipes and tasks. These images are stored on our servers to be displayed to other members of your household.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): We use push notifications to alert you about task reminders, new assignments, or updates within your household.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our application.
  • Create and manage your user account and household connection.
  • Synchronize data (tasks, meals, etc.) in real-time between you and your partner.
  • Send you emails or notifications regarding account security, updates, or service-related announcements.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others.

  • Household Sharing: The core function of the app is to share data. Information you add to a shared Household is visible to the other member(s) of that Household.
  • Service Providers: We may share generic aggregated demographic information with our business partners and trusted affiliates (e.g., cloud hosting services like AWS/Google Cloud used to store your data).
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy.

Your Right to Delete Data

In compliance with Google Play Store policies, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

  • In-App: You can delete your account directly within the application settings by navigating to Settings > Manage Household > Delete Account. This action is irreversible.
  • Web Request: If you cannot access the app, you may request account deletion by contacting us at the email provided below.

5. Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information and strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13.
